APP REVIEW — Notes for the reviewer

App name: Andes Padel

  Password: (any password you choose)

What the app does:
  Lets padel players see live court availability at Andes Padel Club,
  book a court, pay by card (Stripe) or bank transfer, and sign up for
  matches, tournaments, leagues and academy classes.

Why it might look empty on first launch:
  - Courts and time slots come from the live backend at
    https://andespadel.yachaq.io/api/
  - Bookable slots are published by club staff for upcoming days. If
    you review outside opening hours, the court list is still visible
    under "Inicio", but slot times may appear on the next screen.

Payments:
  Card payments are processed by Stripe in test-or-live mode. A bank
  transfer option exists where the user uploads a transfer receipt
  (photo) for club staff to confirm.

Push notifications:
  Used only for booking confirmations and match reminders. Requires
  no special entitlement beyond aps-environment (already in the
  provisioning profile).

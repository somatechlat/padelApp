APPLE APP PRIVACY — "App Privacy" questionnaire answers
Verified against the code 2026-10-03.

Data Used to Track You
  → None
     (No IDFA, no advertising SDK, no cross-app tracking.)

Data Linked to Identity
  • Name                          — App Functionality, Account
  • Email Address                 — App Functionality, Account
  • Phone Number (optional)       — App Functionality, Account
  • Other User Content (transfer-proof photos) — App Functionality
  • Purchase History / Payments   — App Functionality
  • Identifiers (user id)         — App Functionality

Data Not Linked to Identity
  • Diagnostics (crash logs)      — App Functionality

NOT collected — do not tick these
  • Usage Data / Analytics        — firebase_analytics is NOT a dependency
  • Location                      — the address shown is the club's, not the user's
  • Health & Fitness, Contacts, Browsing History, Search History
  • Financial info (card numbers) — handled entirely by Stripe; never touches
                                    our servers or the app

Third-party SDKs
  • Stripe (payments)             — Purchase History / Payment info.
                                    Card numbers go card → Stripe; we never see them.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging      — push token (Identifiers), crash Diagnostics
  • firebase_analytics            — NOT bundled. If it is added later this
                                    questionnaire must be updated first.

Data Sharing
  → No. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.
  Photos of transfer receipts are visible only to club staff who confirm the
  payment.

Data Deletion
  → YES, offered in-app: Perfil → Borrar cuenta (Delete account).
    POST /api/gdpr/me/erase/ anonymizes the record and blacklists the session
    tokens. Also available by request: info@loyallia.com

Age range
  → 4+ (sports booking; no gambling, no unrestricted UGC).
